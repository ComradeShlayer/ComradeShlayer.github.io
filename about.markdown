 I am currently a master’s student at the University of British Columbia in Vancouver working with Dr. Heather Bruce in the ancient homology lab. I grew up in Mississauga, Ontario near the Rattray marsh. As a kid, I always wanted to be a scientist and took it upon myself to conduct my own surveys of the local fish during their annual spring migration upstream. I completed my undergraduate degree at Carleton University in Ottawa. When the stars align and I have free time I enjoy spending it with my cat Pippin, crocheting, watching F1 and doing crossword puzzles.
